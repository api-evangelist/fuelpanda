# FleetPanda

> FleetPanda is a cloud-based dispatch and delivery platform for fuel distributors — "the operating system for fuel distributors." It manages operations across tank wagon / wet hosing, truck & trailer, transport, lubricants, and propane delivery, with dispatch, order management, same-day billing, reconciliation, a driver mobile app, and a customer portal. FleetPanda is SOC 2 Type 1 compliant (June 2026) and is backed by 500 Global.
